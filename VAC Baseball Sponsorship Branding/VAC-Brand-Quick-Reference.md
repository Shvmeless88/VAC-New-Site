# Vehicle Approval Centre — Brand Quick Reference

Pulled from vehicleapprovalcentre.com for the baseball team sponsorship.

## Logo

- **vac-logo-color-on-white.png** — full-color mark + "VAC" wordmark, for use on light/white backgrounds (e.g. jersey sleeve, light banner).
- **vac-logo-white-on-dark.png** — reversed white mark + wordmark, for use on dark backgrounds (e.g. navy jersey, dark signage).
- **vac-social-share-graphic.jpg** — full brand graphic with tagline, for reference on colors/type pairing.

## Colors

| Swatch | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary periwinkle/indigo | `#7380FF` | Main brand color (site theme color) |
| Deep navy/indigo | `#1E1F4D` | Dark background |
| Mid indigo | `#2C3175` | Dark background gradient |
| Light lavender | `#C0C6FA` | Accent text / highlight on dark backgrounds |

## Typography

- Headings/body: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (bold weights for headings), with Outfit as a secondary display font.
- Both are free Google Fonts if the printer/embroiderer needs the typeface.

## Wordmark / Name

- Short form: **VAC**
- Full name: **Vehicle Approval Centre**
- Tagline: "Your Next Vehicle, Delivered to Your Door."
